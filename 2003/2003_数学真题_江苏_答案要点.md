**答案：存在。当 $a > \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 时，$E\left(0, \dfrac{a}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{4a^2 - 2}}{4}\right)$，$F\left(0, \dfrac{a}{2} + \dfrac{\sqrt{4a^2 - 2}}{4}\right)$，定值为 $a$；当 $a = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 时，$E, F$ 重合于点 $\left(0, \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\right)$，定值为 $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$；当 $0 < a < \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 时，$E\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt{2 - 4a^2}}{4}, \dfrac{a}{2}\right)$，$F\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{2 - 4a^2}}{4}, \dfrac{a}{2}\right)$，定值为 $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$。**
