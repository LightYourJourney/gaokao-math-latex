\gksubpart{（1）}

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}

\nopagebreak[4]

\gksubpart{（2）}

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}

\nopagebreak[4]

\gksubpart{（3）}

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
前 $n$ 个奇数的和为 $n^2$，故

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}
\frac{1+3+\cdots+(2n-1)}{n(n+1)}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac n{n+1}=1.
\]

\gksubpart{（4）}

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
\left(\sqrt[3]x-\frac1{\sqrt x}\right)^8

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
\frac{8-k}{3}-\frac k2.

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
令其等于 $1$ 得 $k=2$，系数为

\nopagebreak[4]
\[

\gksubpart{（5）}

\nopagebreak[4]
\[

\]

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
所以

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
3\le x\le5.
\]

\gksubpart{（6）}

\textbf{解析}

\gksubpart{（1）}

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
z^3=\cos3\theta+i\sin3\theta,\qquad
\overline z^{\,3}=\cos3\theta-i\sin3\theta.
\]

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
所以

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
z^3+k\overline z^{\,3}
=(1+k)\cos3\theta+i(1-k)\sin3\theta.
\]

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
若按源解答把纯虚数限定为非零纯虚数，则须满足

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
(1+k)\cos3\theta=0,\qquad
(1-k)\sin3\theta\ne0.
\]

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
因此参数条件可写为

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
\boxed{
\begin{cases}
k=-1,\quad \theta\ne\dfrac{n\pi}{3};\\[4pt]
\text{\text{或} }k\ne1,\quad
\theta=\dfrac\pi6+\dfrac{n\pi}{3},
\end{cases}
\quad n\in\mathbb Z.}
\]

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
当 $\theta$ 变化时，

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
\begin{aligned}
|z^3+k\overline z^{\,3}|^2
&=(1+k)^2\cos^23\theta+(1-k)^2\sin^23\theta\\
&=1+k^2+2k\cos6\theta.
\end{aligned}
\]

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
故最大值和最小值分别为

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
\boxed{1+|k|},\qquad
\boxed{|1-|k||}.
\]

\nopagebreak[4]
\[
\begin{aligned}
AB^2
&=(CE-CD)^2+AD^2+BE^2\\

\cos\angle ACB
=\frac{2^2+3^2-7}{2\cdot2\cdot3}
=\frac12,

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
